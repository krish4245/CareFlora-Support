Privacy Policy

A transparent, plain-English overview of what Careflora collects, how end-to-end encryption protects you, and our zero-advertising commitment.

Support Guide Privacy Policy App Overview

Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

Careflora ("Careflora," "we," "us") is committed to protecting your privacy while delivering a delightful botanical care experience. We collect only the minimum data required to power your plant collections, task reminders, and community interactions. We do not sell your personal data, nor do we run third-party advertising or behavioral tracking networks.

2. Information We Collect

The following table outlines all categories of data processed by Careflora:

Account Credentials Your name, email address, and securely hashed password. When requesting password resets, temporary 6-digit OTP codes are generated.
Profile & Plant Data Optional avatar photo, plants in your collection, custom watering/fertilizer schedules, and completed task history.
Community Posts Photos, captions, comments, likes, and bookmarks you voluntarily publish to the Community feed. Visible to other registered members.
End-to-End Encrypted Chat Private direct messages between users. Encrypted on-device using X25519 & AES-256-GCM; our servers relay encrypted ciphertext only and cannot read message content.
Local Sandbox Cache 3D RealityKit plant models and cached gallery images stored locally on your iPhone for offline rendering and performance.
Device & Compatibility Basic hardware checks (e.g. LiDAR scanner or A12 Bionic processor availability) to enable AR capabilities without crashing. No advertising IDFA is collected.

What We Never Collect: Careflora does not collect precise continuous GPS locations, access your phone contacts/address book, record microphone audio, or collect financial/credit card details.

3. How Your Information Is Used

  • To authenticate your account securely and maintain your user session.
  • To calculate personalized watering, misting, and repotting reminders via iOS notifications.
  • To deliver Community posts and allow likes/comments between growers.
  • To relay end-to-end encrypted private messages between devices.
  • To send account recovery emails and one-time password reset passcodes.
  • To ensure compatibility with RealityKit AR features on your iPhone.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Careflora does not include third-party advertising SDKs, ad trackers, or marketing analytics tools. To operate the app, we utilize standard cloud infrastructure vendors bound by strict confidentiality and data-protection terms:

  • Cloud Database & API Servers: Securely stores user accounts, plant profiles, and community posts under TLS encryption.
  • Media CDN: Distributes high-resolution plant imagery and 3D RealityKit models with fast global delivery.
  • Transactional Email Dispatch: Sends authentication and password reset codes directly to your inbox.

5. End-to-End Encrypted Chat (E2EE)

Careflora's direct messaging system is designed with zero-knowledge cryptography. Using elliptic-curve X25519 key exchange and AES-256-GCM payload encryption, conversations are locked and unlocked exclusively on the participating devices. Our servers cannot read, decrypt, or log your conversation content.

6. Local Storage & iPhone Sandbox

To reduce cellular data usage and allow offline viewing, Careflora caches your plant lists and downloaded AR models in your iPhone's protected application sandbox. This cached data remains solely on your device and is permanently deleted when you remove Careflora from iOS.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

While plant care is a wholesome, family-friendly hobby, Careflora incorporates open community feeds and direct peer-to-peer messaging. Therefore, Careflora is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has registered without your consent, please contact us immediately at leafora070511@gmail.com, and we will promptly delete the account and associated records.

8. Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

You may request permanent deletion of your account, plant schedules, and community posts at any time. Simply email leafora070511@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We process and purge all records within 48 hours.
