All-in-One Plant Companion

Careflora is your all-in-one plant care companion built natively for Apple iPhone. Visualize any plant in your space with AR before you buy, then keep it thriving with smart task reminders for watering, feeding, and repotting. Search plants and get personalized care recommendations, browse garden tips, and connect with a community of plant lovers by sharing posts, likes, comments, and saves. Chat privately with end-to-end encrypted messaging.
