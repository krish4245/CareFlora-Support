About Careflora

Careflora is your all-in-one plant care companion built natively for iPhone. Visualize any plant in your personal space using true-to-scale RealityKit Augmented Reality before you buy, keep your indoor jungle thriving with automated care reminders for watering, feeding, and repotting, discover expert tips in the Garden Hub, and connect with fellow growers through private end-to-end encrypted messaging.

Troubleshooting & Common Questions

AR view won't open or camera preview is black.
RealityKit AR plant visualization requires a physical iPhone equipped with a LiDAR scanner (iPhone 12 Pro or newer) or an Apple A12 Bionic chip or newer. It is not supported on the iOS Simulator or older devices. Ensure camera permissions are granted under Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → Careflora.
Password reset one-time passcode (OTP) not arriving.
Check your Spam or Junk folder. Automated security emails may occasionally be filtered. Reset codes expire after 10 minutes. If your code expired, tap "Resend Code" in the app or reach out to our team at leafora070511@gmail.com.
Task reminders and watering alerts are not appearing.
Verify that notifications are enabled under iOS Settings → Notifications → Careflora, and that an active Focus Mode (Do Not Disturb or Sleep) is not silencing your alerts. Also verify that care intervals are set on your plant's profile.
Chat messages are not sending or appear unsynced.
Careflora direct chat uses hardware-accelerated end-to-end encryption (X25519 key exchange + AES-256-GCM). Both users must connect to the internet to complete the initial cryptographic key handshake. Try closing and reopening the app if a conversation appears out of sync.
3D plant models or community photos take time to load.
AR plant models and photos stream the first time you view them to minimize initial app size, then cache safely in your iPhone's local sandbox for instant offline rendering. Subsequent views load immediately.
